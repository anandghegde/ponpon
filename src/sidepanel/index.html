PonPon

Turn every pawn into gold

PonPon reviews your chess.com games with Stockfish running on this computer. Every move gets a label, and the best moves show as arrows on the board. Open a game, finished or in progress, the analysis board or a bot game, then this panel. Reviews are unlimited, and nothing leaves your computer.

During your own games

Suggestions and arrows show during games in progress too — yours as well as ones you're only watching. Know the risk: chess.com's Fair Play Policy bans engine help during play and closes accounts for it, even in unrated games. PonPon stays off only on pages it can't read: variants, puzzles and lessons, or a page it doesn't recognise.

    About

    PonPon . Stockfish 19 runs on this computer, and nothing is sent anywhere. Labels use Lichess's Win% model: how much each move changes your winning chances.
